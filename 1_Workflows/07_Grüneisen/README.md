
## Calculation of the Grüneisen Parameter (QHA)

**Use:** Obtain the mode Grüneisen parameter on the canonical bandpath through a quasi-harmonic approach.

**!Attention!** Obtaining the Grüneisen parameter from third-order force constants is also possible and, depending on your systems, potentially preferable, follow the workflow outlined in [03_Phono3Py](https://github.com/Kit553/PhononTools/tree/master/1_Workflows/03_Phono3Py) for the corresponding workflow.
